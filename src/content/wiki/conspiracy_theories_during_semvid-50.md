## The Virus as a Biological weapon

Shortly after first cases of [SEMViD-50](semvid_virus) in [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) became known, a new conspiracy theory emerged in podcasts and social media channels, claiming that SEMViD-50 was a bioweapon and purposefully placed in Switzerland by a Russian terror organization. More specifically, golden eagles released into the wild were said to previously have been infected with SEMViD to spread the disease across Europe.

The conspiracy theory was backed by contemporary rumors that the virus could not unfold in Russia due to the colder climate, and that Russian experts had already successfully developed a vaccine against SEMViD and would thereby protect their own population.

Regarding Russia’s motives, theory supporters claimed that Russia was trying to weaken Western states to reduce resistance against invasions into Ukraine and neighboring EU member states. Others maintained that Russia’s pharmaceutical industry was set to massively profit from the sale of an alleged vaccine. Both theories were strongly supported by the Russian decision to not invite WHO experts into national territory and not share infection numbers with the international community.

While most people believed the official narrative regarding its origin, the theory that SEMViD-50 was a Russian bioweapon gained noteworthy traction across multiple sides of the political spectrum.

Portraying Russia as a dangerous aggressor state, conservative hawks and frightened citizens widely spread demands for military and economic sanctions across social media. Simultaneously, those sympathizing with Russia were eager to voice calls for unification and collaboration. Backed by the speculative Russian vaccines, the SEMViD-50 pandemic was idealized as an opportunity to weaken US influence in Central Europe.

Because both Western military officials and the Russian state denied the bioweapon theory, it failed to gain traction as a plausible origin story. Clear public information on platforms like “semvid.info” further bolstered public trust in the official narrative.

## The Virus as a Reason for Misuse of blood transfusions

A theory quickly spread through society that the virus was merely a pretext to boost the willingness to donate blood. The underlying claim was that wealthy individuals sought to use the blood for rejuvenation and health improvement; they would supposedly “filter” the blood supplies based on viral load and inject themselves with the seemingly contaminated units—which were, in fact, perfectly usable. This was alleged to be a devious scheme: the resulting severe shortage of blood supplies would prompt even more people to donate, thereby providing these wealthy individuals with a greater supply of “rejuvenation material” and effectively exploiting the public’s empathy. Bill Gates was a particular target of the theory’s proponents.

Since the vast majority of the blood transfusions examined were indeed administered to patients, the core claim of the conspiracy theories could be quickly refuted.

## Religious Interests

During the pandemic, allegations were leveled against Jehovah’s Witnesses claiming they sought to generally prevent the donation and transfusion of blood. These claims stemmed from the group’s belief that human blood is sacred and must not be transferred to others; consequently, it was alleged that they were using the virus as a pretext to reduce blood donations. Furthermore, it was claimed they were propagating the idea that the use of sanitary facilities inevitably results in individuals carrying a certain viral load, thereby rendering blood donation impossible. In this way, they were accused of exploiting the virus to align the population more closely with their faith. A secondary effect of this narrative, it was argued, was to encourage people to lead chaste lives and abstain from extramarital sex—to avoid mucosal infection—thereby serving the Jehovah’s Witnesses’ goal of guiding people toward a life more pleasing to God.

In fact, the point where blood reserves approached zero was never reached. Scientists also vehemently rejected the claim that the general population more or less automatically carried a certain viral load. Consequently, it was quickly demonstrated that this theory was highly contradictory. Furthermore, the number of Jehovah’s Witnesses did not see any significant increase.
